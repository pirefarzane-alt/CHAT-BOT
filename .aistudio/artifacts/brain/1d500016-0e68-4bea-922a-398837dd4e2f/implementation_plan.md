# hamed - Persian AI Chatbot & Messenger Assistant

hamed is an intelligent Persian AI chatbot designed for natural Persian conversation and language learning. It features a multi-platform messenger UI switcher (supporting Telegram, WhatsApp, and Bale aesthetic modes), customizable conversational tones, real-time sentiment analysis badges, interactive Persian language insights, voice message transcription, voice speech playback (TTS), and an interactive **Live Voice Conversation** mode powered by Gemini and Firebase.

## 1. Overview & Core Concept

- **What It Does**: hamed functions as an AI Persian conversation companion and tutor. Users can ask questions on any topic, have live voice conversations, practice conversational Persian, receive personalized feedback, customize hamed's response tone, analyze emotional tone, and experience the chat inside their favorite Iranian and global messenger themes (Telegram, WhatsApp, Bale).
- **Target Audience / Persona**: Persian speakers, Persian language learners, students, and professionals seeking a culturally fluent, emotionally attuned AI companion accessible via recognizable messaging formats.
- **Key Value**:
  - **Live Voice Conversation**: Talk directly with Hamed in real-time with instant transcription and spoken answers.
  - **Voice Audio Playback**: Listen to any of Hamed's responses on-demand with one tap or automatically.
  - **Chat History Preserved**: Every spoken turn during live voice conversation is immediately saved and synced to the chat history.
  - **Culturally Fluent Persian**: Natural Persian syntax, idiomatic expressions, and proverbs.
  - **Multi-Platform Versatility**: Instant toggle between Telegram (signature blue/cloud styling), WhatsApp (emerald green styling), and Bale (Iranian messenger blue/orange modern styling).
  - **Emotionally Intelligent Responses**: Live sentiment tags that modulate hamed's empathy and tone.
  - **Tone Customization**: Switch between Formal (رسمی), Friendly/Casual (صمیمی), Witty/Humorous (شوخ‌طبع), Poetic/Literary (شاعرانه), Academic/Precise (علمی و دقیق), and Educational (آموزشی).
